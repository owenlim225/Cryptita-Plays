Cryptita Plays is a community-driven social impact initiative focused
on bridging Web3 education and social development for underserved
communities in the Philippines. Founded with the belief that access to
knowledge should not be limited by location, income, or internet
availability, Cryptita Plays works to make digital literacy, blockchain
awareness, and emerging technology education accessible, safe, and
beginner-friendly.
The initiative was established in response to the growing digital divide,
especially in rural and hard-to-reach areas where access to quality
educational resources and internet connectivity remains limited.
Cryptita Plays addresses this gap by combining traditional learning
tools such as books and storytelling with simplified introductions to
Web3 concepts, ensuring that learners are exposed to future
technologies in a responsible and age-appropriate manner.
At its core, Cryptita Plays operates with a strong education-first
mindset. The goal is not to encourage immediate adoption of
blockchain technologies, but to build early familiarity and awareness
so that students and communities are prepared when these
technologies become part of everyday life. By focusing on
foundational understanding, critical thinking, and digital safety, the
initiative empowers individuals to make informed decisions in the
future digital economy.
Cryptita Plays collaborates closely with educational institutions,
community leaders, foundations, and Web3 organizations that share
the same mission of ethical education and long-term impact.
Through these partnerships, the initiative continues to expand its
reach while maintaining transparency, integrity, and a strong
community-centered approach.
Programs and Initiatives
Mini-Library Mission and Outreach Program
Cryptita Plays establishes community-based mini libraries in
underserved areas, providing children and students with access to
educational books, Web3 learning materials, and QR-based digital
resources. These libraries serve as safe learning spaces where curiosity
is encouraged and learning continues even without reliable internet
access.
Web3 Education and University Programs
The initiative conducts university seminars, workshops, and community
learning sessions focused on blockchain basics, digital safety, career
awareness, and responsible Web3 participation. These programs aim
to introduce concepts clearly and honestly, helping students
understand opportunities and risks in the digital space.
Web3 Learning Materials
Cryptita Plays develops beginner-friendly learning tools such as the
Barya to Blockchain: Young Learners Encyclopedia, Web3 activity books,
and storytelling materials designed for children and youth. These
resources simplify complex concepts while promoting responsible
digital behavior.
ACIS: Adopt-a-Child Iskolar Program
The ACIS Program supports selected students from Mini-Library
communities by providing monthly educational assistance, along with
school supplies and learning materials. Each Mini-Library area selects
five iskolar beneficiaries to help reduce financial barriers, improve
school participation, and encourage long-term educational growth.
Founder
Cryptita Plays was founded by Arshelene R. Lingao, a Web3
community builder and social impact advocate with extensive
experience in blockchain education and community engagement. Her
work focuses on youth empowerment, inclusive education, and
creating safe, values-driven learning environments. She is also an
active supporter of women-led initiatives in technology and education,
believing that access and representation are essential for sustainable
growth.
Cryptita Plays stands as a bridge between education, technology, and
social impact. Through its programs, partnerships, and communitydriven approach, the initiative continues to create meaningful
opportunities for learning and growth. By prioritizing education over
hype and people over technology, Cryptita Plays remains committed to
building a future where every community has the tools and knowledge
to thrive in a digital world.
