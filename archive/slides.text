SLIDE 2: WHO WE ARE
Cryptita Plays is a community-driven social impact initiative focused on making education,
digital literacy, and Web3 awareness accessible to youth and underserved communities
across the Philippines.
We believe learning should be simple, safe, and inclusive.
SLIDE 3: THE PROBLEM
Many communities face:
• Limited access to quality educational resources
• Lack of digital literacy and Web3 awareness
• High exposure to misinformation and online risks
• Financial challenges that affect school continuity
Education gaps often widen opportunities lost.
SLIDE 4: OUR APPROACH
We address these challenges through:
• Education-first programs
• Community-based learning
• Safe and beginner-friendly Web3 awareness
• Consistent and long-term support
Our goal is not immediate adoption, but familiarity, confidence, and preparedness.
SLIDE 5: CORE PROGRAMS
Mini-Library Mission
Providing books, learning kits, and Web3 materials to underserved communities.
Replenish Mini-Library Program
Returning to communities to restock books and introduce new learning resources.
Web3 Education Seminars & Workshops
Beginner-friendly talks on blockchain, digital safety, and Web3 careers.

SLIDE 7: NEW PROGRAM – ACIS
Adopt a Child Iskolar Program (ACIS)
ACIS provides selected students with:
• Monthly educational assistance
• School supplies and learning materials
• Access to Mini-Library resources
Up to 5 students per Mini-Library area are selected to receive consistent support.
SLIDE 8: HOW ACIS HELPS
ACIS helps students by:
• Reducing financial stress on families
• Supporting regular school attendance
• Encouraging focus on learning
• Building confidence and motivation
Beneficiaries commit to learning, participation, and being positive role models in their
community.
