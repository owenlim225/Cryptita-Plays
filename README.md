# Cryptita Plays

Cryptita Plays is a community-driven social impact initiative bridging Web3 education and social development for underserved communities in the Philippines.

## About

Cryptita Plays was founded on the belief that access to knowledge should not be limited by location, income, or internet availability.
The initiative delivers beginner-friendly digital literacy, blockchain awareness, and emerging technology education through safe, ethical, and community-centered programs.

## Mission

To make digital and Web3 education accessible, safe, and practical for underserved communities by combining grassroots outreach, inclusive learning tools, and responsible technology awareness.

## Vision

A future where every community has the tools, confidence, and foundational knowledge to thrive in the digital economy.

## Why We Exist

The initiative responds to the widening digital divide, especially in rural and hard-to-reach areas where quality educational resources and internet access are limited.
Cryptita Plays addresses this challenge by combining traditional learning methods (books, storytelling, community mentoring) with clear and age-appropriate introductions to Web3 concepts.

## Programs And Initiatives

### Mini-Library Mission And Outreach Program

Community-based mini libraries provide children and students access to educational books, Web3 learning materials, and QR-based digital resources.
These spaces support consistent learning even in areas with unreliable internet.

### Web3 Education And University Programs

University seminars, workshops, and community learning sessions cover blockchain basics, digital safety, career awareness, and responsible Web3 participation.

### Web3 Learning Materials

Beginner-friendly tools such as:
- **Barya to Blockchain: Young Learners Encyclopedia**
- Web3 activity books
- Storytelling materials for children and youth

These resources simplify complex concepts while promoting responsible digital behavior.

### ACIS: Adopt-a-Child Iskolar Program

The ACIS Program supports selected students from Mini-Library communities through monthly educational assistance, school supplies, and learning materials.
Each Mini-Library area selects five iskolar beneficiaries to help reduce financial barriers and improve school participation.

## Founder

Cryptita Plays was founded by **Arshelene R. Lingao**, a Web3 community builder and social impact advocate focused on youth empowerment, inclusive education, and safe, values-driven learning environments.
She is also an active supporter of women-led initiatives in technology and education.
