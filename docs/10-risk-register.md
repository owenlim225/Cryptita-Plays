# 10 Risk Register

## Purpose

Track and manage project risks for delivery and operations.

Related docs: [Product Requirements](./01-product-requirements.md), [Release and Deployment](./08-release-and-deployment.md), [Maintenance and Operations](./09-maintenance-and-operations.md)

## Risk Scale

- Likelihood: Low / Medium / High
- Impact: Low / Medium / High

## Active Risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Delayed approval of sponsor-facing copy | Medium | High | Timebox reviews and define approvers early | Product | Open |
| R-02 | Incomplete impact metrics at launch | Medium | Medium | Publish verified baseline metrics; label as initial | Product | Open |
| R-03 | CTA channel not actively monitored | Low | High | Assign SLA and backup contact owner | Ops | Open |
| R-04 | Accessibility gaps in final UI | Medium | High | Add QA gate with keyboard/contrast checks | Engineering | Open |
| R-05 | Brand inconsistency across assets | Medium | Medium | Use approved design tokens and asset checklist | Design | Open |

## Review Cadence

- Review weekly during build.
- Review monthly after launch.
- Escalate high-impact risks immediately.
