# 03 Information Architecture

## Purpose

Define the one-page structure for a sponsor-facing site that presents Cryptita Plays, its programs, impact, and partnership pathways in a clear conversion flow.

Related docs: [Master Context](./00-master-context.md), [Content Strategy](./02-content-strategy.md), [Design Brief](./04-design-brief.md), [Technical Specification](./05-technical-specification.md)

## Audience

- Potential sponsors and partners
- Foundations and education collaborators
- Community supporters and volunteers

## One-Page Anchor Structure (Slide-Aligned)

- `#hero` - Cover / positioning
- `#who-we-are` - Organization intro
- `#the-problem` - Community challenges
- `#our-approach` - Education-first model
- `#core-programs` - Mini-Library, Replenish, Web3 education
- `#learning-materials` - Curriculum and youth tools
- `#acis` - Adopt-a-Child Iskolar Program
- `#acis-outcomes` - How ACIS helps students and families
- `#impact` - Reach and social proof
- `#partners` - Ecosystem collaborators
- `#why-this-matters` - Mission rationale
- `#whats-next` - Near-term expansion priorities
- `#get-involved` - Sponsorship and collaboration CTAs
- `#contact` - Contact channels
- `#thank-you` - Closing reinforcement

## Navigation Model

- Sticky top nav with section anchors.
- Mobile menu with same anchors.
- Persistent primary CTA in header: `Support the Mission`.
- Secondary CTA in strategic sections: `Partner With Cryptita Plays`.

## Content Hierarchy Rules

- One H1 in hero.
- One H2 per major section.
- H3 used for program cards and support pathways.
- Keep paragraphs short and scannable for sponsor review.
- Place CTA blocks after value-heavy sections (programs, ACIS, impact).

## Sponsor-First Story Flow

1. What Cryptita Plays is and whom it serves.
2. Why the problem is urgent in underserved Philippine communities.
3. How the initiative works (education-first, safe, beginner-friendly).
4. What programs already exist and how they operate.
5. Why ACIS is a practical sponsorship entry point.
6. What impact has been achieved through consistency and partnerships.
7. What support is needed next and how to engage immediately.

## Section Content Requirements

- **Who We Are:** community-driven initiative; simple, safe, inclusive learning.
- **The Problem:** limited educational access, low digital literacy, misinformation/online risk, financial barriers.
- **Our Approach:** no hype-led adoption; build familiarity, confidence, and preparedness.
- **Core Programs:** Mini-Library Mission, Replenish Mini-Library Program, Web3 seminars/workshops.
- **Learning Materials:** Barya to Blockchain encyclopedia, activity books, coloring/storytelling, digital safety materials.
- **ACIS:** monthly educational assistance, supplies/materials, access to Mini-Library resources, up to 5 students per area.
- **Impact:** active communities, thousands reached, multi-sector partnerships, continuous support.
- **Partners:** educational and community collaborators.
- **Get Involved:** sponsor programs, volunteer/collaborate, partner for expansion, support safe learning spaces.

## UX and Accessibility Notes

- Logical tab order.
- Landmark semantics (`header`, `main`, `section`, `footer`).
- Visible focus states and sufficient color contrast.
- Readable type scale and spacing for fast scanning.
- Alt text for all meaningful community/program imagery.
