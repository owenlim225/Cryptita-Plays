# 03 Information Architecture

## Purpose

Define one-page content structure, hierarchy, and anchor navigation.

Related docs: [Content Strategy](./02-content-strategy.md), [Design Brief](./04-design-brief.md), [Technical Specification](./05-technical-specification.md)

## Page Structure

- `#hero`
- `#about`
- `#programs`
- `#impact`
- `#partnerships`
- `#contact`

## Navigation Model

- Sticky top nav with section anchors.
- Mobile menu with same anchors.
- Persistent primary CTA button (`Sponsor With Us`).

## Hierarchy Rules

- One H1 in hero.
- One H2 per major section.
- Optional H3 for sub-program cards.
- CTA appears in hero, partnerships, and footer/contact.

## Content Prioritization

1. Mission and trust signals.
2. Program relevance and delivery model.
3. Impact indicators and social proof.
4. Partnership options and clear next step.

## Wireframe Notes

- Hero: headline, supporting copy, CTA.
- Programs: card grid.
- Impact: stats strip + narrative block.
- Partnerships: sponsor pathways cards.
- Contact: high-visibility form or direct channels.

## Accessibility And UX

- Logical tab order.
- Landmark semantics (`header`, `main`, `section`, `footer`).
- Visible focus states and sufficient color contrast.
- Avoid content walls; keep scan-friendly spacing.
