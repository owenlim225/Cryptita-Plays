# 12 Project Management

## Purpose

Define execution, ownership, and reporting for delivering a concise one-page sponsor-facing Cryptita Plays website aligned to approved source content and slide flow.

Related docs: [Master Context](./00-master-context.md), [Implementation Plan](./06-implementation-plan.md), [Risk Register](./10-risk-register.md), [Decision Log](./11-decision-log.md)

## Delivery Principles

- Content fidelity to approved source documents.
- Sponsor-first clarity: fast understanding, clear trust signals, direct action paths.
- Keep scope focused on one-page launch readiness.
- Prioritize ACIS and core program communication without overloading the page.

## Scope Baseline (In)

- One-page site architecture and responsive UI.
- Slide-aligned narrative sections from cover to thank-you CTA.
- Core programs, educational materials, impact, partners, and ACIS.
- Contact and conversion pathways for sponsors/collaborators.

## Scope Baseline (Out)

- Multi-page CMS rollout.
- Donation/payment processing integrations.
- Unapproved impact claims or new program statements.

## Workstreams

- **Content:** rewrite and approval of section copy from source-of-truth files.
- **Design:** wireframe + visual system + responsive mockups.
- **Engineering:** one-page implementation with anchor navigation and accessible UI.
- **QA:** content accuracy, responsive checks, accessibility and CTA flow validation.
- **Launch:** deployment readiness and post-launch monitoring baseline.

## Roles and Ownership

- **Project Lead:** timeline, cross-functional coordination, stakeholder updates.
- **Content Lead:** source alignment, final copy approval, claim consistency.
- **Design Lead:** visual quality, hierarchy, and sponsor-focused UX.
- **Engineering Lead:** implementation quality, performance, and accessibility.
- **Partnership Liaison:** partner logo/data accuracy and contact pathway readiness.

## Milestones

- **M1 - Content Lock:** IA, design brief, and page copy approved.
- **M2 - Design Lock:** responsive mockups and component guidance approved.
- **M3 - Build Complete:** one-page implementation complete in staging.
- **M4 - QA Pass:** content, accessibility, and responsive checks signed off.
- **M5 - Launch Ready:** go-live checklist completed and owner handoff done.

## Recommended Timeline (Lean)

- Week 1: Content lock + IA confirmation.
- Week 2: Design lock.
- Week 3: Implementation + internal QA.
- Week 4: Final review, fixes, and launch.

## Quality Gates

- All claims and program details match source-of-truth text.
- ACIS details are present and correctly framed (up to 5 students per Mini-Library area).
- Anchor navigation works across device sizes.
- WCAG AA contrast and keyboard navigation pass.
- CTAs are visible in hero, mid-page, and closing sections.

## Risk Watchlist

- Content drift from approved source documents.
- Scope creep beyond one-page sponsor site.
- Missing or outdated partner references.
- Late approvals affecting launch sequence.

## Change Control

- Any scope/content/timeline change must be logged with impact.
- Content changes affecting claims require Content Lead approval.
- Design/engineering scope changes require Project Lead + Engineering Lead sign-off.
- Final decisions recorded in [Decision Log](./11-decision-log.md).
