# 01 Product Requirements

## Objective

Build a one-page public website that helps sponsors and partners quickly understand Cryptita Plays and take action to collaborate.

Related docs: [README](./README.md), [Master Context](./00-master-context.md), [Content Strategy](./02-content-strategy.md), [Information Architecture](./03-information-architecture.md), [Technical Specification](./05-technical-specification.md)

## Product Context

Cryptita Plays is a community-driven social impact initiative focused on bridging Web3 education and social development for underserved communities in the Philippines. The site must communicate this clearly and convert attention into sponsor conversations.

## Target Audience

- Corporate CSR leads
- Foundations and grant teams
- Web3 ecosystem partners
- Community collaborators
- Individual supporters

## User Problem

Potential sponsors need a credible, concise overview of mission, programs, impact, and partnership options. Today this information is fragmented across materials.

## Business Goals

- Increase qualified sponsor/partner inquiries.
- Improve trust and clarity for first-time visitors.
- Standardize public-facing narrative across outreach channels.

## Non-Goals (Phase 1)

- Multi-page content hub
- Donation/payment portal
- Complex CMS workflows
- Member login area

## Required One-Page Sections

1. Hero (mission + clear CTA)
2. Who We Are
3. The Problem
4. Our Approach
5. Core Programs
6. Educational Materials
7. ACIS Program
8. Impact
9. Partners and Collaborators
10. Why We Do This
11. What’s Next
12. Join / Contact

Section content details and copy priorities: [Content Strategy](./02-content-strategy.md)

## Functional Requirements

- Responsive one-page layout (mobile-first).
- Primary CTA visible in hero and repeated near footer.
- Contact block includes:
  - `cryptitaplays@gmail.com`
  - `@cryptitaplays`
  - `+63-906-0925-761`
- Clear section anchors for fast navigation.
- Basic SEO metadata and social preview tags.

## Content and Tone Requirements

- Education-first, mission-aligned, sponsor-facing.
- Beginner-friendly language; avoid technical overload.
- No hype framing; emphasize responsible learning and long-term support.
- Keep copy concise and skimmable.

## Success Metrics

- Sponsor/contact CTA click-through rate
- Number of qualified inquiries per month
- Scroll depth to partner and contact sections
- Time on page from sponsor-intent traffic

## Acceptance Criteria

- All mandatory program names and partner lists appear exactly as required.
- One-page narrative flows from mission -> problem -> approach -> programs -> impact -> partnership CTA.
- Contact pathways are visible and functional.
- Final copy is aligned with source text and ready for sponsor review.
