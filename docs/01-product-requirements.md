# 01 Product Requirements

## Objective

Build a one-page sponsor-facing site that explains Cryptita Plays mission, programs, impact model, and partnership opportunities to increase qualified sponsor inquiries.

Related docs: [Content Strategy](./02-content-strategy.md), [Information Architecture](./03-information-architecture.md), [Technical Specification](./05-technical-specification.md)

## Problem Statement

Potential sponsors need a fast, credible overview of Cryptita Plays but currently lack a single, structured touchpoint optimized for trust and conversion.

## Goals

- Present mission and social impact with clarity.
- Showcase flagship initiatives and outcomes.
- Convert visitors into sponsor inquiries.
- Provide a professional and trustworthy digital presence.

## Non-Goals

- Multi-page marketing site.
- Full donor portal or payment processing.
- Complex CMS at initial launch.

## Target Users

- Corporate CSR managers.
- Foundations and grant officers.
- Web3/community partners.
- Individual high-intent sponsors.

## Success Metrics

- Sponsor inquiry submissions per month.
- Contact CTA click-through rate.
- Time on page and section scroll depth.
- Bounce rate from sponsor-focused traffic.

## Core Requirements

- One-page narrative with clear sections.
- Mobile-first responsive experience.
- Primary CTA: sponsor/contact action.
- Easy-to-update copy and key metrics.
- Baseline SEO and share metadata.

## Constraints

- Small team and short launch timeline.
- Content should be easy to maintain without heavy tooling.
- Messaging must remain mission-accurate and ethical.

## Open Questions

- Which inquiry channel is primary (email, form, calendar)?
- Which impact metrics are approved for public use at launch?
- Are testimonials/partner logos ready for publication?
