# 09 Maintenance and Operations

## Purpose

Define ongoing ownership and operational practices after launch.

Related docs: [Release and Deployment](./08-release-and-deployment.md), [Risk Register](./10-risk-register.md), [Project Management](./12-project-management.md)

## Ownership

- Product/content owner: approves copy and metrics updates.
- Engineering owner: maintains site reliability.
- Communications owner: monitors inquiry channel response.

## Recurring Tasks

- Monthly content/metric review.
- Quarterly link and accessibility audit.
- Dependency/security updates on agreed cadence.

## Monitoring

- Track uptime and deployment alerts.
- Monitor inquiry volume and CTA conversion.
- Review analytics for drop-off sections.

## Incident Response (Lightweight)

- Detect issue via monitoring/user report.
- Triage severity and assign owner.
- Apply fix or rollback.
- Record outcomes in [Decision Log](./11-decision-log.md).

## Documentation Hygiene

- Update `docs/00-master-context.md` first for any change to facts, claims, messaging, partner names, or contact details.
- Propagate approved updates from `docs/00-master-context.md` to dependent docs in this folder.
- Treat `company background.txt` and `slides.text` as archival/reference-only inputs, not maintained canonical sources.
- Update [Risk Register](./10-risk-register.md) for new concerns.
- Archive outdated assumptions explicitly.
