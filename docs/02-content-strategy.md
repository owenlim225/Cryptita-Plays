# 02 Content Strategy

## Purpose

Define concise, sponsor-facing messaging for the Cryptita Plays one-page website, aligned to approved source content.

Related docs: [README](./README.md), [Master Context](./00-master-context.md), [Product Requirements](./01-product-requirements.md), [Information Architecture](./03-information-architecture.md), [Design Brief](./04-design-brief.md)

## Messaging Direction

- **Primary message:** Bridging Web3 Education and Social Impact
- **Brand promise:** Empowering youth and underserved communities through education, digital literacy, and care.
- **Positioning:** Education-first, safe, beginner-friendly, community-driven.

## Narrative Flow (One Page)

1. **Mission hook**  
   Cryptita Plays as a bridge between education, technology, and social impact.
2. **Problem clarity**  
   Resource gaps, digital literacy gaps, online risks, and financial barriers.
3. **Approach**  
   Community-based, education-first, responsible Web3 awareness.
4. **Programs**  
   Mini-Library, seminars/workshops, learning materials, ACIS support.
5. **Impact + credibility**  
   Concrete approved claims and named collaborators.
6. **Partnership invite**  
   Clear ways to support, collaborate, and connect.

## Section-by-Section Copy Intent

### Hero
- Headline: `Cryptita Plays`
- Subheadline: `Bridging Web3 Education and Social Impact`
- Supporting line: `Empowering youth and underserved communities through education, digital literacy, and care.`
- CTA: Sponsor/Partner with us

### Who We Are
- Short paragraph on community-driven mission in the Philippines.
- Reinforce "simple, safe, and inclusive" learning.

### The Problem
Use these points:
- Limited access to quality educational resources
- Lack of digital literacy and Web3 awareness
- High exposure to misinformation and online risks
- Financial challenges that affect school continuity

### Our Approach
Use these points:
- Education-first programs
- Community-based learning
- Safe and beginner-friendly Web3 awareness
- Consistent and long-term support

## Voice and Style Rules

- Plain, confident, sponsor-ready language.
- Keep sections short and scannable.
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Avoid technical jargon and hype terms.
- Keep focus on people, education, and long-term outcomes.

## Implementation Notes for Content Handoff

- Keep each section self-contained for easy component mapping.
- Ensure headings mirror navigation labels.
- Lock mandatory claims, partner names, and program names as non-editable content tokens during implementation.
- Reuse exact wording from this doc and [Product Requirements](./01-product-requirements.md) for final QA.
