# 02 Content Strategy

## Purpose

Define messaging structure and content priorities for a sponsor-facing one-page site.

Related docs: [Product Requirements](./01-product-requirements.md), [Information Architecture](./03-information-architecture.md), [Design Brief](./04-design-brief.md)

## Messaging Pillars

- Mission-first social impact.
- Practical education programs.
- Community trust and safety.
- Transparent collaboration opportunities.

## Narrative Flow

1. Hook: why the mission matters now.
2. Credibility: who Cryptita Plays serves.
3. Programs: what is delivered on the ground.
4. Impact model: expected outcomes and indicators.
5. Sponsor pathway: how partners can engage.
6. CTA: direct inquiry/contact step.

## Section Content Plan

- Hero: mission statement + primary CTA.
- About: context and values.
- Programs: mini-library, Web3 education, learning materials, ACIS.
- Impact: measurable indicators and stories.
- Partnership: sponsor types and support options.
- Contact: email/social/contact number + final CTA.

## Content Standards

- Clear, plain English; minimal jargon.
- Evidence-based claims only.
- Tone: warm, credible, community-centered.
- Accessibility: short paragraphs, meaningful headings.

## Asset Requirements

- Program photos (rights-cleared).
- Founder and team profile snippets.
- Partner logos (if permitted).
- Approved impact indicators.

## Governance

- Content owner: project lead.
- Reviewers: founder + program representative.
- Update cadence: monthly or when metrics change.
